These WAV samples are original, mathematically synthesized sounds generated by
scripts/sounds.mjs. They contain no third-party recordings or sampled audio.

The active palette is deliberately selective:
- Theme changes use tactile toggle-on and toggle-off samples.
- Explicitly enabling sound plays enable-sound; muting is immediate and silent.
- Tag pills have a quiet hover pop, with no navigation-click sound.
- Social-icon hover accents match the icon:
  YouTube: play-button click; Twitch: chat pops; GitHub: soft paw taps;
  LinkedIn: connection chime; Stack Overflow: stacking clicks;
  Bluesky: a stylized butterfly wing flutter; Mastodon: a low soft puff;
  email: an envelope-like paper flap; RSS: a broadcast ping.

These are synthesized suggestions of those textures, not recorded animals,
paper, broadcast audio, platform notification sounds or brand sound marks.
Hover previews stop on pointer exit and never add a separate click cue.
Ordinary navigation, card bodies, keyboard focus, search, sharing, dialogs,
newsletter submission and installation/update controls remain silent.

The samples are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

This dedication applies only to these WAV samples, not to the rest of the repository.
Older versioned sample directories remain for compatibility with cached clients.
