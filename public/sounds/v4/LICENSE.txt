Each recording is converted to 22,050Hz mono signed 16-bit PCM, faded in for
5ms and out for 20ms, and peak-normalised to 0.08 before the app's gain of 0.25.
No platform notification sounds or brand sound marks are included.
Attribution is not required by CC0, but the creators are credited above.

ORIGINAL CONTROL SOUNDS

toggle-on.wav, toggle-off.wav, enable-sound.wav and tag-hover.wav are original
mathematical synthesis, dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal.
They are unchanged from v3. They contain no third-party recordings.

REGENERATION AND PLACEMENT

Run node scripts/sounds.mjs from the repository root with Node.js and FFmpeg on
PATH. This requires network access to the three public source downloads.
Only asset regeneration needs FFmpeg; the app, build and tests do not.
The script verifies the original SHA-256 hashes before decoding, applies the
edits above and writes all seven WAVs. It fails if a download, hash or decode
fails. No original full-length recordings are deployed.
The recorded edits were generated with FFmpeg N-120424-g03b9180fe3-20250731.

Only GitHub, Bluesky and Email have icon hover sounds. YouTube, Twitch,
LinkedIn, Stack Overflow, Mastodon and RSS remain silent.
Hover requires a 100ms mouse dwell, stops on pointer exit/down, and never adds
an activation sound. Touch and keyboard focus are silent.
Existing theme, explicit sound-enabling and tag-hover feedback is retained.
Ordinary navigation, cards, search, sharing, dialogs, newsletter submission
and installation/update controls remain silent.

Older versioned directories remain unchanged for cached clients. The current
app requests only v4 assets; old synthetic social accents are no longer used.
