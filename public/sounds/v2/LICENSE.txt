These WAV samples are original, mathematically synthesized sounds generated by
scripts/sounds.mjs. They contain no third-party recordings or sampled audio.

The palette uses layered oscillators, inharmonic partials and deterministic
filtered noise for tactile switches, plucked navigation, wooden card previews,
paper-like page turns, water-like tags, metallic social previews, coffee bubbles,
sharing bells, submission sweeps and confirmation chords.

They are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

This dedication applies only to these WAV samples, not to the rest of the repository.
