Newsletter footer icons
======================

These are existing icon glyphs rasterised to transparent 48x48 PNG files,
with a charcoal (#242424) colour and consistent padding. No logo paths were
redrawn. AVIF companions are supplied for repository image conventions; email
templates must use the PNG files for compatibility. Trademark rights remain
with their respective owners; the icon licences do not grant trademark rights.
